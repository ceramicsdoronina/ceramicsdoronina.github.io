Privacy notice

Information on the processing of personal data pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 (“Privacy Code”).

1. Data controller

Ceramics Doronina, Via San Donato 17R, 16123 Genova (Italy). Contact for any privacy request: ceramist.doronina@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

When you buy a product, book a masterclass or buy a gift card we process:

  • first and last name, e-mail address, phone number;
  • billing address and, for physical products, shipping address;
  • the content of the order (items, quantities, amount, number of participants and date of a masterclass);
  • the choices you make on the order page (the four choices described in section 5), with date and time and the version of the notice shown.

Payment details (card number, etc.) are entered directly on the Stripe page. They do not pass through our website and we do not store them.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Carrying out the order: confirming payment, preparing and delivering products, managing masterclass bookings (including sending a calendar invitation to the e-mail address you provide) and answering your requests. Legal basis: performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations: tax and accounting. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Information by e-mail in the future, other contact channels and sharing of data with third parties for surveys and market research: only according to the choices described in section 5. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Providing the data needed to carry out the order is necessary: without it we cannot complete the order.

4. Recipients and providers

Your data is not sold. It is processed by providers acting on our behalf, including:

  • Stripe, for payments and the checkout page;
  • Netlify, for the functions that create the payment and receive its confirmation;
  • Google (Sheets, Calendar, Apps Script, Gmail), for the order record, masterclass bookings and e-mail;
  • GitHub Pages, for hosting this website;
  • the order notification service used by the shop, which alerts us to each new order;
  • couriers and tax or accounting advisers, as far as necessary.

Some providers may also process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer takes place with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Only if you select the fourth choice (section 5) may the data also be shared with third parties for surveys and market research.

5. Consents and choices on the order page

On the order page you will find four choices:

  1. Order processing: necessary for the contract and mandatory. Without this choice the order cannot be completed.
  2. Information by e-mail: it is pre-selected on the order page. If you leave it selected, we keep the order data and use it to send you information by e-mail in the future. If you deselect it, ceramicsdoronina has no right to use your data to contact you in the future. This choice relies on your explicit decision and you can withdraw it at any time by writing to ceramist.doronina@gmail.com.
  3. Other channels (phone, messaging apps, social networks): only if you tick it yourself; it is not pre-selected.
  4. Sharing of data with third parties for surveys and market research: only if you tick it yourself; it is not pre-selected.

You can withdraw any consent at any time by writing to ceramist.doronina@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

6. Retention

Order and billing data is kept for as long as tax and accounting obligations require (normally 10 years, Art. 2220 of the Italian Civil Code). For information by e-mail and other channels, the data remains usable until you withdraw your consent. When the period ends, or on withdrawal, data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you can object to the processing (Articles 15–22 GDPR) by writing to the controller’s address. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it). There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses technical cookies and the browser’s local storage (localStorage) to remember your cart, your language and your cookie choices. Google Analytics (with IP address anonymisation) and the Meta pixel are loaded only if you accept the relevant banner, and not before.
